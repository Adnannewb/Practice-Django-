About JUST PhysioSphere

A university-based center dedicated to restoring movement, independence, and quality of life.

Our history

JUST PhysioSphere was founded as a collaborative initiative between the Department of Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation and the Medical Center of Jashore University of Science and Technology.

What began as a small clinic serving campus staff and students has grown into a comprehensive rehabilitation center with multiple specialties, research collaborations, and community outreach programs.

Mission & vision

Our mission is to provide safe, accessible, and evidence-based physiotherapy that empowers patients to take an active role in their recovery.

  • Deliver excellent patient‑centered care.
  • Advance physiotherapy practice through research.
  • Develop the next generation of rehabilitation leaders.

Chairman’s message

“Movement is not just about muscles and joints; it is about dignity, participation, and joy. At JUST Physiotherapy center we treat every patient with the same care we would offer to our own families.

Our therapists are handpicked for their clinical expertise, curiosity, and kindness. We continuously review our protocols against the latest research to make sure that every visit moves you closer to your goals.”

Approach to care

We follow an active, education‑focused approach. Instead of passive modalities alone, we emphasize therapeutic exercise, movement education, and self‑management strategies.

At each visit you will know:

  • Your diagnosis and the factors contributing to it.
  • The plan for today’s session and upcoming visits.
  • What you can do at home to speed up your recovery.

Our goal is for every patient to leave the clinic with more confidence, less pain, and a clear roadmap.
