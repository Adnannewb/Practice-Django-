Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about appointments, pricing, cancellations, and what to expect at JUST PhysioSphere.

No, a doctor’s referral is not required for most appointments at JUST PhysioSphere. However, if you have recent imaging (X‑ray, MRI, etc.) or a prescription from a doctor, please bring it with you. For complex medical conditions we may coordinate with your physician for your safety.

  • Comfortable clothing that allows you to move easily.
  • Any medical reports, imaging, or prescriptions related to your condition.
  • Your student ID (if you are using the student email discount).
  • A list of medicines you are currently taking.

Most sessions are between 45–60 minutes, depending on your treatment type. The first visit includes a detailed assessment; follow‑up visits focus more on treatment and exercise. The exact duration you see in the Services page is the booking time reserved for you.

Yes. You can cancel or reschedule directly from your dashboard up to 24 hours before the appointment start time.

  • If you paid online, refunds are processed automatically when you cancel in time.
  • If it is less than 24 hours, the system will prevent changes and you may be charged.
  • Therapists can also cancel with a refund if they become unavailable.

You will receive notifications via the email address used during registration for:

  • New appointment confirmations.
  • Rescheduled or cancelled appointments.
  • Refund confirmations for online payments.

You can always see the latest status in your dashboard under “Upcoming Appointments”.

If you register with an email ending in @student.just.edu.bd, the system automatically marks you as a student.

  • First visit: 200 TK
  • Each additional visit: 100 TK

For all other emails, standard community pricing applies: 300 TK for the first visit, then 200 TK for subsequent sessions. The correct amount is calculated automatically whenever you book.
